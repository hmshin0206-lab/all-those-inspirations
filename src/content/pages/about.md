Hi, I’m **Heather Shin**, a global marketer and product strategist passionate about building meaningful digital experiences across borders.

This blog began as a class assignment during my master’s at Johns Hopkins. I was taking a course on Design Leadership, and one of the deliverables was to reflect on what we’d learned. So, I opened this little corner of the internet, thinking it would be a one-off post.

But something stuck.

Since then, this space has grown with me, across industries and roles, from building consumer tech at LG, to scaling messaging platforms at LINE+, to leading Web3 initiatives at Kaia. Each chapter has shaped how I think about product, growth, and storytelling, and through it all, I’ve kept coming back to writing: to reflect, to refine, and to connect.

Today, I’m reviving this blog as a living portfolio and a personal thinking hub. You’ll find:

• Reflections on product, brand, and growth strategy

• Industry analysis and marketing experiments

• Notes on career transitions, working internationally, and life in motion

Thanks for stopping by. I hope this space sparks ideas, connection, and inspiration.

📍 Based in Seoul (for now).
