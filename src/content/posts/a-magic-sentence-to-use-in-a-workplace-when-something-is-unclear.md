Today, a developer who is working on a feature of which I’m in charge asked a question. And here is the Slack message that I received.

![](/images/posts/a-magic-sentence-to-use-in-a-workplace-when-something-is-unclear/01-image-3.png)

Ok… I don’t fully understand what he means. I don’t know the part *“the username size is dynamic”* because it’s too technical. Based on the context, though, I’m dimly aware that he has a problem using the current localization key which is defined in a specification document that I worked on.

Here is how I responded.

![](/images/posts/a-magic-sentence-to-use-in-a-workplace-when-something-is-unclear/02-image-4.png)

<small class="caption">I got three “thumbs up”!</small>

So the magic sentence here is **“Let me clarify if my understanding is correct.”** This can be replaced with other sentences with the same connotation such as **“Let me double-check if I understood your request correctly”** or **“Can you confirm if my understanding is correct?”**.

If you were looking for something that is totally unexpected, sorry to disappoint you. But this truly is a secret sauce that can be used in any situation when you think you didn’t get it 100% right. Come to think of it, I have used this sentence not only in the workplace but on many occasions. For example, while I was studying for my masters in the U.S., every lecture, team project, and even networking session which were covered in English was such a huge challenge. So I used this sentence many times to overcome the blurriness due to my imperfect English. I will call it a **clarification or recapitulation process**.

Here are the reasons why this is important.

1.  Through recapitulating, you can organize your understanding. There is a famous quote by Albert Einstein saying, *“If you can’t explain it simply, you don’t understand it well enough.”*
2.  Even if your understanding is wrong or slightly off, there is a chance that you get nice feedback from your coworkers because they already know that you tried hard enough to comprehend.
3.  And most importantly and without a doubt, it is always better to check on something before you blunder of which the damages far outweighs.

If you are still not sure if it works or not, why not giving it a try? 😌
