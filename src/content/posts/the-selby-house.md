# The Selby House by Todd Selby

This exhibition displays various forms of art; from photographies and installation arts to drawings.  
In the photography section, people – regardless of what they are doing for a living – is the main theme. Selby tries to show each individual’s different aspects of life and their definitions of art reflected in their own space, mostly in their houses or offices.

In the drawing and installation art section, he uses his own imagination based on his past experience, for instance his visit to New York City and Las Vegas, as well as a family trip to Papua New Guinea in which he was completely fooled by his father and thought that he was going to Hawaii until he took the first step out to this exotic (from his perspective) country.

I guess what he tries to convey through this exhibition is that an art, as it sounds very professional and prestigious, is actually everywhere and within everyone. Also, I felt that his use of vibrant colors lightens the weight of life that sometimes makes us feel miserable, and cheers us up instead. I bought a postcard and two colorful masking tapes as a souvenir.
