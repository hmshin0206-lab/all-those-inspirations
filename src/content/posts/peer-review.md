My company has an official peer review twice a year. And the result of the first half was sent through my email yesterday.

![](/images/posts/peer-review/01-image.png)

Fortunately, I did not get any harsh feedback. There were two comments that expected me to make the best use of data and locals’ voices, which can contribute to an increase in the performances of future promotions, but other than that, there weren’t any serious complaints or words that might deteriorate my reputation. (I’m proud to share that my boss told me that it was the best evaluations among the ones she has been reading! *(Updated on July 30)*)

Speaking of a peer review system, I think this is a lot better than just getting a review from a superior. In my previous company, the evaluation was solely done by one’s manager. Since I have been the youngest in my teams for 5 years in a row and the company structure was quite hierarchical, there were not many chances that I got to speak with my team leaders directly; My ideas were often indirectly delivered to the boss through middle managers. Here, I’m in the middle management position in my team but looking back, I assume some of my bosses in the past did not fully understand what I was doing and thus my evaluations might have been negatively affected.

As for the peer review system, however, you get quite a number of feedback not only from your boss, but also from the colleagues who are working closely with you and thus know you well. If you are the supervisor, you get to understand their strengths and weaknesses from different perspectives, which will help you be less biased when you evaluate this person. Even if there is a colleague who always has a conflict with you and he/she left a negative comment with evil intent while the others are all positive, there is little chance that you get low marks. Also, since the feedback is anonymous, there is no worry to make personal or make a big fuss about it.

This is definitely one of the many things that I like about my company. I am grateful that I am working with awesome people (not because they left nice comments about me (HAHA)). I will keep trying my best to make this year be filled with joy and fulfillments both personally and professionally.
