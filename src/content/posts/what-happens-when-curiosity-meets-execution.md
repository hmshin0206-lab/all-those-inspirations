Hi. I’m Heather! It’s been a while since I last wrote here. In 2016, I started this blog as a space to collect thoughts, loosely structured around my work and life in tech and content. I never expected it to grow into anything particular — and to be honest, it didn’t. Until now.

Lately, I’ve been thinking more about *transition*.

Not just career transition — though yes, I’m actively navigating one — but mental shifts: from operating to leading, from Asia-focused to global-first, from marketing what others build to shaping what gets built.

As someone who’s worked across product, partnerships, and growth strategy at companies like LINE, LG, and Kaia (Klaytn), I’ve always sat at the edge of **tech and storytelling**. And recently, with AI reshaping how we work, create, and think, I’ve felt a stronger urge to document that edge — to make sense of it out loud, and maybe help others do the same.

So here’s what you’ll find in this blog going forward:

• Notes on **product and growth strategy** — from campaigns I’ve led to ones I admire

• Reflections on **AI, tools, and future-of-work trends**

• Behind-the-scenes of my ongoing **career transition into global tech & AI**

• Thoughts on **collaboration, clarity, and what makes meaningful work**

In short: a space where curiosity meets execution.

Welcome (back), and thanks for reading. Let’s see where this goes.
