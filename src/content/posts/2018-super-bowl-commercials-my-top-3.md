**Amazon “Alexa Loses Her Voice”**

Amazon’s Super Bowl commercial has been a hit since they released its teaser a few days before the actual Super Bowl game. The ad starts with a very creative and intriguing question, ‘What happens if Alexa (Amazon’s AI speaker) loses her voice?’. They starred Jeff Bezos, the CEO of the company, which rendered a sense of reality as well as raised people’s expectations. Also, they assigned world-famous celebrities such as Gordon Ramsay, Cardi B, Rebel Wilson, and Anthony Hopkins in the perfect position in the scene, making it hilarious but attractive.

No. 2

**Doritos & Mountain Dew “The Best Song of Ice and Fire”**

I didn’t know that Doritos and Mountain Dew are owned by the same company (Pepsi.Co) until I watched this ad, and I think this is a superb way of advertising two different products from a single company.  And in terms of delivery, Pepsi did it smart; the company made a striking contrast between the two products by presenting Doritos as fire (red) and Mountain Dew ice (blue). The rap battle of two well-known Hollywood stars – Peter Dinklage and Morgan Freeman – was also fun to watch. Right after the Super Bowl, they launched the Snapchat filter that enables users to mimic the rap in the ad, which will definitely boost users engagement.

No. 1

**Tide “It’s All The Tide Ads”**

I haven’t watched all the Tide ads before this one, but I bet this is the best ever! Tide cast David Harbour, who became a superstar after the Netflix Original ‘Stranger Things’. In the beginning, it seems to show typical ads for typical items – car, beer, perfume, diamond, beverage, and mattress etc. – but they turn out to be the Tide ads. How amazing and ingenious the idea is!
