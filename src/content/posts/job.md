I have been asking myself constantly about which industry and what kind of company I should work for. There are many ways to categorize the companies: a giant conglomerate vs. a startup (or mid-sized company if allowing a third option), an agency vs. a client, or a Korean based vs. a foreign company.

A giant conglomerate like my previous company must be well-organized, strong in finance but have a higher chance of a rigid company culture. A startup, on the contrary, should be young and agile, but might be lacking stability. By working in an agency, you will have a chance to experience various and dynamic projects with broader spectrum but sometimes have to be dragged to a direction that what the client wants even though you disagree. Also, foreign-affiliated firms in Korea are normally regarded to have horizontal culture compared to the companies founded in Korea (except for a few techs or IT companies). However, you might not have a full control of the business and have to follow the guideline created by the HQ.

I admit that this is a generalization and there is a logical leap, but some aspects might still be applicable. Therefore, I had to be very selective in terms of a job application so far. I do not have a specific guideline in mind yet, but I have some wishes of the companies, regardless of sizes, I would love to work to have:

-   High potential to grow
-   An emphasis on employees’ growth as well
-   High value in moral righteousness
-   Fast decision-making process (no inefficient and redundant reports)
-   The culture that everyone regardless of positions can speak up and be appreciated

A job, to me, is a path to accomplish what I aim for or intend to be in my life. Hence, I hope to find a joy and fulfillment from the work I do. Some people express their worries for me that I should not narrow the opportunities that are just out there. Yet still, I believe in vocation, meaning that the right job eventually will find the right person.

Meanwhile, I’m currently spending my time fully devoting to myself – like doing 2~3 hours of yoga every day, reading books that I have been procrastinated, and cooking a healthy meal etc. I’m truly grateful that I have this little pause in my life to think back and forth. Also, I’m grateful that I do not feel anxious or agitated as I used to be and rather cherish this precious moment.
