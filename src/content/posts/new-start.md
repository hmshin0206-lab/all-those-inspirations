I just sent my regret letter to another company that offered me a job. I guess I just made one of the most difficult decisions in my life and my heart is still pounding. I expected the next step after the graduate study would be the turning point in my career and thus, I desperately sought for the right answer. (I wonder if there is one though.)

Both companies are very well known. One is a consulting firm that has over decades of history and has a global awareness. But its profit has been stagnant for the past couple of years. Another is relatively new (4~5 years old) but has been growing super fast and has a recognition in some countries mostly in Southeast Asia, not much in Korea though.

In order to get information about these two companies, I not only searched online but also contacted several people who are currently working in these two companies and asked questions. Also, I made my Pros & Cons list of each company. It made my decision even harder since both companies are good and their pros and cons are almost opposite; for instance, the con of the company A was the pro of the company B. Lastly, I also asked the opinions of a few of my peers and seniors but their preferences were split in half so it didn’t do much of a help.

Making a long story short, I decided to choose the latter, the younger company. There are several reasons but first, I prefer the company whose business is growing since it affects the company culture and boosts the employees’ morale. Also, I wanted to pursue my career as a marketer, even I admit working as a consultant challenges a person to be skillful and to grow to the maximum level, making the job itself very attractive. Yet the most importantly, I thought it suits my expectations on job more. (Please refer to the previous post that I wrote about my contemplation on job: [Job](https://heathershinblog.wordpress.com/2017/10/22/job/))

It is too early to conclude whether I made the right choice or not but at least I feel lucky that I had these great options to choose. And I am excited about the new job and the people I will meet soon.

Merry Christmas!
