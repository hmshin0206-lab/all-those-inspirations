-   Where: D Museum Hannam

-   During: May 3, 2018 ~ Nov. 25, 2018

This exhibit features a collection of different artists on a single theme, ‘weather’. For some, the weather is a means to reflect their states of mind, while it can also be used to express their ideologies. If you watch the videos below, you can see that every installation and the disposition of each piece was carefully calibrated and deeply calculated. And the background music went so well with the atmosphere.
