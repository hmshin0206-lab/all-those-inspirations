I remember that I was fascinated by this video the first time I watched it in one of the optional workshops held in our school before the graduate program started. Though it might have taken tremendous efforts and time to analyze the complexities in the data and render this visualization, it is quite mind-blowing that how easy and clear this sounds and looks like. Having data is not enough; it is also pivotal to “communicate” the data with the generals who are not professional data analysts. And I believe that this should be one of the marketer’s roles as well.
