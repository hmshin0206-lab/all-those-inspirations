One of the many advantages of my new role is that the coverage of marketing is very broad. For example, in my previous company, I was in charge of developing some of the products’ marketing creatives. My specific job was to strategize and plan content but turning them into more aesthetically appealing output was passed to designers’ hands. In other words, there was a clear distinction between the role of a client and that of an agent, and it was ok if I do my part only as a client. However, here, I can get a glimpse of a marketing process from a holistic view since I should take care of literally A to Z; to plan, develop ideas, marketing research (if necessary), communicate with other teams, execute, gather data, and report the result. And in the process, ‘design’ takes a huge part.

So here comes the second merit: there is no limit or boundary for the team in order to get brilliant ideas. Last week, we had to design a prototype of our proposing feature that we suggest to be embedded inside the company’s app and the team leader let us visit the library nearby and see if there are any ideas or inspirations that we might be able to develop and use. So we spent the whole afternoon that day at the library reading magazines and design books. Below are some visual inspirations that I took pictures of. I selected these pictures for specific reasons but I decided not to write every single detail here since then it is going to be too long, might hinder and bias others’ thoughts, and there might be a confidential issue as well. I hope these pictures to be a little bit of a help stimulating visual inspirations for those who found this page while surfing and searching for ones.
