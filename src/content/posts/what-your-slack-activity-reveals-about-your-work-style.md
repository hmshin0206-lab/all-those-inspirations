I asked our team’s Slack AI a lighthearted question: “Based on my activity, what animal would I be?”

The answer came back immediately: **a busy bee** 🐝.

The reasoning was surprisingly specific. The bot analyzed my recent messages and identified patterns I hadn’t consciously named:

![](/images/posts/what-your-slack-activity-reveals-about-your-work-style/01-frame-1.png)

What I appreciate most about this small moment is that it came from an unexpected source. Not from a performance review, not from a self-assessment framework, but from an AI analyzing behavioral patterns in everyday work.

Sometimes the clearest mirrors aren’t the ones we deliberately look into. They’re the ones that reflect what we actually do, not what we think we do or what we aspire to do.

If you have access to Slack AI or similar tools, I’d recommend trying this exercise. Ask what patterns emerge from your actual behavior. You might be surprised by what your daily work reveals about your strengths—and what kind of value you’re creating without even realizing it.
