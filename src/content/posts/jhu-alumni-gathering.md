Posing the “Korean finger heart” all together

It is always an inspiration to meet new people in a common circle that binds us and see how different our courses of lives are. To be honest, during my stay in the U.S., I tried to keep a distance from Korean groups because I wanted to throw myself to a completely new environment all by myself and make the best use of it, especially in maximizing my English proficiency. However, after coming back to Korea, I realized that it would have been great if I got to know some group of people from my home country since network plays a huge role when you get older and more experienced. And not only this practical reason, having someone who can understand your difficulties as a foreign student, just because they have already gone through it, would be a huge comfort as well. Thus, I was glad that I could participate in the school gathering this time. And I thought I should join this kinds of meetups more often.
